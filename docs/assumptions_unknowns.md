The foundation predated the sponsor pack. [VDR-01](data_recon/01_dataset_inventory.md) was accepted and integrated through [PR #7](https://github.com/Slave-of-Skynet/training_agrifood/pull/7), [VDR-02](data_recon/02_temporal_leakage.md) was accepted and integrated through [PR #14](https://github.com/Slave-of-Skynet/training_agrifood/pull/14), VLD-02A ([ADR 0002](decisions/0002-predictive-input-semantics.md)) was accepted and integrated through [PR #16](https://github.com/Slave-of-Skynet/training_agrifood/pull/16), [VDR-03](data_recon/03_target_horizon_feasibility.md) was accepted and integrated through [PR #18](https://github.com/Slave-of-Skynet/training_agrifood/pull/18), and [APR-01](product_recon/04_evidence_map.md) (steps 0–5) was delivered and integrated through [PR #17](https://github.com/Slave-of-Skynet/training_agrifood/pull/17). This reconciliation records their measured observations and research evidence separately from unresolved production policies and analytical/product semantics. It updates the earlier pre-profiling state without treating the supplied snapshot as a guarantee about future data.

## Working assumptions limited to this foundation

- Python 3.11+ and Node.js 20.19+ or 22.12+ are local development prerequisites.
- The demo runs batch/on-demand through HTTP.
- The fixture is synthetic and exists only to prove contract transport.

These are implementation conditions for the skeleton, not claims about challenge data or agricultural operations.
