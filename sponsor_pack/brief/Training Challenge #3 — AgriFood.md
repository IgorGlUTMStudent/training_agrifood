# AgriFood Challenge

## Smart Harvest: Reduce Post-Harvest Losses

Farmers and agricultural businesses can lose a significant part of harvested produce because of inappropriate storage conditions, delayed transportation or poor inventory decisions.

Temperature, humidity, harvest dates, storage information and previous loss records may already be available, but problems are often discovered only after product quality has deteriorated.

### Challenge

Develop an intelligent solution that uses agricultural and storage data to help farmers identify produce at risk and reduce post-harvest losses.

Participants will receive sample data containing crop batches, harvest information, storage conditions, environmental measurements, transportation status and historical losses.

The solution should help a farmer or storage operator understand:

- which batches are most at risk;
- when product quality may begin to deteriorate;
- what factors contribute to the risk;
- what action should be prioritized to reduce potential losses.

Teams are free to choose the technologies, analytical methods and product format they consider most appropriate.

### Evaluation

Solutions will be evaluated based on:

- agricultural and business usefulness;
- accuracy and reliability;
- usability and explainability;
- potential reduction of food loss;
- scalability;
- innovation.

The goal is to transform agricultural data into practical insights that help producers protect product quality and act before losses occur.
